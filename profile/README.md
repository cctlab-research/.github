# Computational Communication Technology Lab

The CCT Lab is led by [Chau Tong](https://chautong.github.io/) at the University of Missouri. We study how search, social platforms, journalism, and AI shape public information in health and politics.

Our research connects AI and algorithmic information systems, health communication and information equity, political communication and journalism, and computational measurement.
